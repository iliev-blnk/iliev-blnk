Full-stack developer in Chișinău. Portfolio and CV: [ilyushavb.pages.dev](https://ilyushavb.pages.dev).

Data and forecasting:

- [holdshort](https://github.com/iliev-blnk/holdshort): Istanbul Airport rebuilt from open ADS-B data. Rust ingest and rules engine, a [browser replay](https://holdshort.pages.dev), landing-time forecasts and approach anomaly detection. [Technical write-up](https://holdshort.pages.dev/writeup).
- [grid.md](https://github.com/iliev-blnk/grid.md): Moldova's electricity load forecast for tomorrow, [published every morning](https://iliev-blnk.github.io/grid.md/) and scored against the grid operator's own plan.
- [cutline](https://github.com/iliev-blnk/cutline): the 2026-27 Champions League [simulated 10,000 times](https://iliev-blnk.github.io/cutline/) after every matchday.

Libraries and pages:

- [parlo](https://github.com/iliev-blnk/parlo): live captions and translation for talks, fully offline. The projector shows captions, phones follow in Romanian, Russian or English. whisper.cpp and M2M100, [benchmarked](https://github.com/iliev-blnk/parlo#benchmark) on FLEURS.
- [pagepad](https://github.com/iliev-blnk/pagepad): scan a QR code and your phone becomes a remote for any web page. On [npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/pagepad), with a [live demo](https://pagepad-demo.pagepad.workers.dev).
- [pagereel](https://github.com/iliev-blnk/pagereel): web pages to video, frame by frame, identical every time. On [npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/pagereel), with a [gallery](https://iliev-blnk.github.io/pagereel/).
- [ink](https://github.com/iliev-blnk/ink): ink in water, drawn in characters. The homepage of my [portfolio](https://ilyushavb.pages.dev).
- [after-dark](https://github.com/iliev-blnk/after-dark): [one night in Chișinău](https://iliev-blnk.github.io/after-dark/) as a long exposure of its streets.
